About

Muhammad Tariq Nadeem

From the factory floor to telecom towers — 20 years building systems that don't fail.

I started my career as an industrial electrician, earning my diploma between 2013 and 2015 and immediately putting it to work in textile plants across Punjab — designing and commissioning machine control panels for spinning and processing machinery.

From there, my career moved into Pakistan's telecom sector. For 15+ years I've supported network operators — Huawei, Jazz, Ufone — maintaining diesel generators, DC power systems, rectifiers, UPS units, and the facility electrical infrastructure that keeps thousands of cell towers online 24/7.

Today I bring that combined expertise — industrial control, telecom power, and panel fabrication — together under Saim Electric Service, building custom panels and providing end-to-end electrical services for industry clients across Pakistan.
